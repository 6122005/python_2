# Usability Notes — Watching Real Users (Fri D85)

> **How to run this:** find 2 people who don't know you built this app.
> Sit with them (or screen-share), give them the URL and password, and
> say only: *"This tool tells you how well your resume fits a job
> posting — try it with a real resume and a real job you're interested
> in."* Then say nothing else. Do not explain the UI. Do not help when
> they hesitate. Write down the moment they pause, frown, re-read
> something, or ask "wait, what does this mean?" — that pause *is* the
> data. Per D86, the top 3 issues you find here become next Monday's
> fix list, and they're almost always one of: confusing labels, unclear
> output format, or no feedback on loading.
