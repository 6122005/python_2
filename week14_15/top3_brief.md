# Top 3 Ideas Brief (Day 68)

## 1. AI WhatsApp HR Assistant (Blue-Collar)
- **Exact User:** A factory floor worker or retail staff member in India who is not fluent in English.
- **Exact Pain:** They want to know their leave balance or HR policies but cannot navigate complex English SaaS apps or find the HR manager on duty.
- **Exact Solution:** A WhatsApp bot that lets them send a voice note in Hindi/Marathi ("mera kitna chutti baki hai?"), and instantly checks the backend database to reply.

## 2. Kirana Store AI Inventory Manager
- **Exact User:** The owner of a local Indian grocery store (Kirana) managing hundreds of SKUs.
- **Exact Pain:** They manually write down missing stock on paper, which is time-consuming and leads to stockouts of high-velocity items.
- **Exact Solution:** A tool where they snap a photo of a shelf, and the AI automatically identifies missing brands and generates a reorder list.

## 3. Independent Plumber/Electrician Quoter
- **Exact User:** An independent local service provider struggling to manage customer inquiries.
- **Exact Pain:** They lose leads because they can't answer calls or give accurate quotes while they are physically working on a different job.
- **Exact Solution:** An AI WhatsApp responder that asks the customer for a photo of the issue, estimates the work, and blocks a calendar slot automatically.
