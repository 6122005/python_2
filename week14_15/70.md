# Validated Problem Statement (Day 70)

## The Problem
India's blue-collar and frontline workforce (over 300 million people) are entirely disconnected from modern HR SaaS platforms because these platforms are built for English-speaking, desk-bound workers. As a result, frontline workers rely on their immediate supervisors for basic HR tasks (checking leaves, understanding policies), which causes massive operational inefficiencies and frustration.

## The Evidence
- *"Aadha time toh chutti ka hisaab karne me nikal jata hai."* — Factory Supervisor
- *"App English me hai, mujhe samajh nahi aata kaha click karna hai."* — Retail Worker
- *"WhatsApp pe likhna time lagta hai, main toh voice message bhejta hu."* — Security Guard

## The Solution
**"VoiceHR"**
An AI-powered HR assistant that lives directly in WhatsApp. It allows frontline workers to ask HR questions, check leave balances, and apply for time off using simple Voice Notes in their native regional languages.
