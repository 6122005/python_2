# Target User Interviews (Day 69)

**Target User Profile:** Factory floor supervisors, retail store managers, and blue-collar workers.
**Goal:** Understand how they currently manage leaves and HR queries.

### Interview 1: Ramesh (Factory Supervisor, Pune)
- **Notes:** Manages 40 workers. Workers constantly come to him during shift changes asking about their PF (Provident Fund) and leave balances. He hates it because he has to check an Excel sheet on the office computer.
- **Quote:** "Aadha time toh chutti ka hisaab karne me nikal jata hai." (Half my time goes into calculating leave balances).

### Interview 2: Sunita (Retail Sales, Mumbai)
- **Notes:** Works at a clothing store. The company uses a portal called "Darwinbox" for leaves. She finds it confusing and usually just asks her store manager to apply for leave on her behalf.
- **Quote:** "App English me hai, mujhe samajh nahi aata kaha click karna hai." (The app is in English, I don't know where to click).

### Interview 3: Anil (Security Guard, Delhi)
- **Notes:** Works 12-hour shifts. He doesn't have an email address. All communication with his contractor is via WhatsApp groups.
- **Quote:** "WhatsApp pe likhna time lagta hai, main toh voice message bhejta hu supervisor ko." (Typing on WhatsApp takes time, I just send voice messages to my supervisor).

### Interview 4: Prakash (HR Manager for a Logistics firm)
- **Notes:** Spends 2 hours every day answering basic questions about company holiday calendars and salary slips over phone calls.
- **Quote:** "If I could just automate the repetitive questions, I could actually focus on hiring."

### Interview 5: Vikram (Delivery Partner)
- **Notes:** Uses an app for deliveries but prefers WhatsApp for personal stuff. Frequently confused about incentive policies.
- **Quote:** "Company policy change hoti rehti hai, koi asaan bhasha me samjhane wala chahiye." (Company policies keep changing, need someone to explain it in simple language).
