# Gap Analysis & Competition Map (Day 67)

| Idea | Existing Competitors (Google/ProductHunt) | Gap in the Indian Market |
|---|---|---|
| **1. AI WhatsApp HR Assistant** | Workday, BambooHR, Darwinbox | Most HR tools are app-based and English-only. Blue-collar workers struggle with them. Voice-first WhatsApp is a massive gap. |
| **2. Kirana Inventory Manager** | Khatabook, Udaan, Jumbotail | Existing apps require manual data entry. None use computer vision on a simple WhatsApp photo to auto-order. |
| **3. AI Legal Drafter** | Cleartax, Vakilsearch | Exist mostly as marketplaces for lawyers. Direct, instant vernacular AI drafting is sparse. |
| **4. Clinic Pre-screener** | Practo, 1mg | Practo is for booking. In-clinic vernucular voice triage is largely untouched. |
| **5. Restaurant Menu Optimizer** | Petpooja, Posist | POS systems track data, but don't actively scrape Swiggy reviews to suggest dynamic pricing. |
| **6. Mock-Interviewer for IT** | InterviewBit, LeetCode | Heavily focused on FAANG. Very few tools focus on the massive volume of mass-recruiters (TCS/Wipro). |
| **7. Regional AI Tutor** | Byjus, Vedantu | Expensive, pre-recorded video courses. Interactive, vernacular AI chatting is still emerging. |
| **8. Compliance Tracker** | RazorpayX Payroll | Existing tools are heavy SaaS. SMB founders just want a WhatsApp alert system. |
| **9. Local Service Quoter** | UrbanCompany | UC is a marketplace that takes a massive cut. Independent plumbers have no automated quoting tools. |
| **10. Voice Expense Tracker** | Splitwise, Zoho Expense | Mostly text/receipt-scan based. Voice-based entry in Hindi/Hinglish is a clear gap. |

**Top 3 Ideas with the biggest gap:** #1 (HR Assistant), #2 (Kirana Inventory), #9 (Service Quoter).
