# 10 AI Ideas for the Indian Market (Day 66)

1. **AI WhatsApp HR Assistant (Blue-collar focus):** An AI bot that allows Indian factory/retail workers to check leave balances and apply for leaves via regional language voice notes.
2. **AI Inventory Manager for Kirana Stores:** A WhatsApp tool that scans a shelf photo and texts the local distributor to restock missing items.
3. **AI Local Legal Drafter:** An assistant that generates basic tenant, vendor, and employment agreements in Hindi and Marathi for small business owners.
4. **AI Clinic Pre-screener:** A voice-bot that talks to patients in waiting rooms in regional languages and summarizes their symptoms for the doctor.
5. **AI Menu Optimizer for Restaurants:** A tool that analyzes Swiggy/Zomato reviews and local ingredient prices to suggest daily specials and pricing updates.
6. **AI Mock-Interviewer for IT Services:** A simulator focused specifically on TCS/Infosys entry-level interview patterns for Tier-3 college students.
7. **AI Tutor for Regional Schools:** A tutor that explains complex science topics using local Indian analogies (e.g. explaining physics using cricket).
8. **AI Compliance Tracker for Startups:** A dashboard that connects to EPFO and GST portals and texts founders when filings are due or mismatched.
9. **AI Plumber/Electrician Quoter:** A WhatsApp bot that allows customers to send a photo of a leak or broken wire, and gives them an instant quote on behalf of the service provider.
10. **AI Freelancer Expense Tracker:** A tool where gig workers can forward voice notes of what they spent ("I paid 500 rupees for petrol") and it auto-categorizes for tax filing.
